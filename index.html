About

Background

[headshot]

An outdoor enthusiast who loves physics too much to leave the engineering world behind. I apply a strong foundation in orbital mechanics, trajectory propagation, and simulation-based state estimation toward guidance, navigation, and control solutions for spacecraft.

My background includes manufacturing engineering work on Sierra Space's Dream Chaser sensor packages — vibration, shock, and thermal vacuum testing — alongside a Teaching Fellow role for CU Boulder's Experimental & Computational Methods course. Outside of engineering, I guide whitewater rafting trips, instruct backcountry skiing, and kitesurf whenever the wind cooperates.
